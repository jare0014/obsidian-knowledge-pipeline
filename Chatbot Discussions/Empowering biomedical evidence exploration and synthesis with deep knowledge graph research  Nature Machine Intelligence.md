## 📝 Summarization
DeepEvidence is a novel deep research agent designed to explore and synthesize evidence from diverse biomedical knowledge sources, using coordinated multi-agent collaboration and constructing an incremental evidence graph. This agent substantially outperforms generic artificial intelligence agents across multiple benchmarks in tasks spanning drug discovery, clinical trial development, and evidence-based medicine. This advancement highlights the significant potential of deep research agents to accelerate biomedical discovery and translational research.
